## Requirements
- xlxs reader (exel , google sheet , ...).
- windows , android , ios , macos device .

## How to get started 
- download the application from our website : https://kendimaher.com/smartbudget/download
- open the file with a xlxs reader .
- implement the categories you spend money on it .
- implement your budget .
- the dashboard will show you the budget balance and the budget satus.
